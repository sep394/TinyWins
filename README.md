# Tiny Wins

Tiny Wins is a private, offline iPhone app for noticing and recording small daily accomplishments.

## Open and run

1. Open `TinyWins.xcodeproj` in Xcode.
2. Choose an iPhone simulator.
3. Press Run.

All data stays on the device in `UserDefaults`. The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising, networking, or third-party dependencies.

## Release checklist

- Replace the placeholder bundle identifier with one registered to your Apple Developer account.
- Choose your development team under Signing & Capabilities.
- Set the version/build number as needed.
- Archive, validate, and upload through Xcode Organizer.
- Add the metadata from `AppStore/Metadata.md` in App Store Connect.
