Privacy Policy for Glimmerstack

Effective August 13, 2026

Glimmerstack is designed to be private. The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

Entries are stored locally on the user’s device. Glimmerstack does not use accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking technologies, or third-party software development kits. The app does not contact external servers.

Deleting an entry removes it from the app. Deleting the app removes its locally stored data, subject to the device owner’s backup configuration and Apple’s device-backup behavior.

This policy may be updated if the app’s functionality changes.

Questions may be submitted through the Glimmerstack support page.
