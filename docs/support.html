Tiny Wins Support

Tiny Wins stores your accomplishments locally on your iPhone. Tap the + button to add a win, use History to revisit entries, and press and hold an entry to delete it.

Frequently asked questions

Does Tiny Wins require an account?
No.

Where is my data stored?
On your device. Tiny Wins does not transmit your entries.

How do I remove an entry?
Press and hold it, then choose Delete.

For additional help, open a support request on GitHub.
